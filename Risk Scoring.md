# Laboratory Risk Scoring

Risk priority is assessed based on two dimensions:

- Likelihood: 1–5
- Impact: 1–5

Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

| Score | Risk Level |
|---:|---|
| 1–4 | Low |
| 5–9 | Medium |
| 10–16 | High |
| 17–25 | Critical |


## Example

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Score | Level |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Data Integrity | 4 | 5 | 20 | Critical |
| Scope & Accreditation | 3 | 5 | 15 | High |
| Personnel Competency | 3 | 3 | 9 | Medium |
| Corrective Action | 4 | 4 | 16 | High |
