# Laboratory KPI Framework

A KPI framework for monitoring the performance, compliance, and risk profile of third-party testing laboratories.

## 1. KPI Objectives

Laboratory KPIs are used to:

- Monitor laboratory compliance and operational performance
- Identify emerging risks and recurring issues
- Track corrective action effectiveness
- Support risk-based laboratory oversight
- Identify areas requiring enhanced monitoring or escalation
- Provide management visibility into laboratory performance

---

## 2. Core Laboratory KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Purpose | Monitoring Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audit Findings | Number of findings identified during laboratory audits | Monitor compliance and control effectiveness | Per audit / Periodic |
| Repeat Findings | Findings repeated from previous assessments | Identify recurring control weaknesses | Periodic |
| Open CAPA | Number of corrective actions currently open | Monitor unresolved compliance issues | Monthly |
| Overdue CAPA | Corrective actions that exceed the agreed due date | Identify delayed remediation | Monthly |
| CAPA Effectiveness | Effectiveness of corrective actions after closure | Verify that issues have been adequately addressed | Periodic |
| Accreditation Status | Current accreditation status and relevant changes | Monitor laboratory authorization and compliance | Periodic |
| Scope Changes | Changes to approved testing scope or capability | Identify potential scope-related risks | Periodic |
| Personnel Competency | Status of required personnel qualifications and competencies | Monitor technical capability | Periodic |
| Testing Errors | Confirmed errors affecting test activities or results | Identify technical and data integrity risks | Periodic |
| Customer Complaints | Relevant complaints associated with laboratory services | Monitor service and operational risks | Monthly |
| Turnaround Time | Time required to complete agreed testing activities | Monitor operational performance | Monthly |
| Risk Level | Current assessed laboratory risk level | Support risk-based oversight and prioritization | Periodic |

---

## 3. KPI Categories

Laboratory KPIs can be grouped into four major categories:

### Compliance

- Accreditation status
- Scope compliance
- Audit findings
- Repeat findings
- Open CAPA
- Overdue CAPA

### Technical Capability

- Personnel competency
- Testing errors
- Technical findings
- Scope and capability changes

### Operational Performance

- Testing turnaround time
- Customer complaints
- Capacity and workload indicators
- Service performance

### Risk & Governance

- Laboratory risk level
- High / Critical risk items
- CAPA effectiveness
- Escalated issues
- Risk reassessment status

---

## 4. KPI-Based Risk Monitoring

KPIs should be reviewed together with the laboratory risk assessment.

Examples:

| KPI Signal | Potential Risk | Possible Response |
|---|---|---|
| Increasing repeat findings | Control effectiveness risk | Enhanced monitoring / audit |
| Overdue CAPA | Corrective action risk | Escalation / management review |
| Accreditation change | Accreditation compliance risk | Scope and authorization review |
| Increase in testing errors | Technical risk | Technical assessment / CAPA |
| Personnel competency gap | Capability risk | Competency review / training |
| Increase in complaints | Service or operational risk | Root-cause analysis |
| Significant scope change | Scope risk | Scope verification |
| Increase in risk score | Overall laboratory risk | Risk reassessment |

---

## 5. KPI Thresholds and Escalation

KPI thresholds should be defined based on laboratory requirements, historical performance, applicable policies, and available evidence.

Where a KPI exceeds an established threshold, possible actions include:

1. Review the underlying data and evidence
2. Identify the potential root cause
3. Assess the impact on laboratory risk
4. Define corrective or preventive actions
5. Increase monitoring frequency where appropriate
6. Escalate significant issues to relevant stakeholders
7. Reassess the laboratory risk level after corrective action

---

## 6. KPI Review Cycle

Laboratory performance should be monitored as part of a continuous governance cycle:

```text
Data Collection
      ↓
KPI Calculation
      ↓
Performance Monitoring
      ↓
Risk Identification
      ↓
Risk Assessment
      ↓
Corrective Action
      ↓
Effectiveness Review
      ↓
Risk Reassessment
```

---

## 7. Management Reporting

A laboratory management review may include:

- Current laboratory risk level
- High / Critical risks
- Open and overdue CAPA
- Repeat audit findings
- Accreditation or scope changes
- Significant technical issues
- Personnel competency concerns
- Customer complaints
- Testing performance
- Major corrective actions
- Issues requiring escalation

The purpose of management reporting is to provide a consistent view of laboratory compliance, operational performance, and emerging risks.

## 9. Risk-Based Governance Model

The KPI framework supports a continuous laboratory governance model:

```text
Laboratory Data
      ↓
KPI Monitoring
      ↓
Risk Identification
      ↓
Risk Scoring
      ↓
Risk-Based Controls
      ↓
Audit / Monitoring
      ↓
CAPA
      ↓
Effectiveness Review
      ↓
Risk Reassessment
      ↓
Management Reporting
```

This approach allows laboratory oversight activities to be prioritized based on risk, performance indicators, and available evidence.
