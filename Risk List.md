# Testing Laboratory Risk Register

A risk-based framework for identifying, monitoring, and managing risks associated with third-party testing laboratories.

Risk List:

1. Impartiality and Conflict of Interest
2. Data Integrity and Fraud
3. Scope Boundary and Accreditation Failure
4. Key Personnel / Competency
5. Safety and Facility
6. Technical Misjudgment / Product Liability
7. Corrective Action Failure
8. Cybersecurity / IP Leakage

| Risk | Risk Indicator | Risk Level | Control | Monitoring | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Data Integrity | Missing raw data / inconsistent records | High | Raw-data traceability | Audit / record review | CAPA / escalation |
| Scope & Accreditation | Testing outside approved scope | High | Scope verification | Periodic review | Stop / corrective action |
| Personnel Competency | Key-person dependency / expired competency | Medium | Competency matrix | Periodic review | Training / replacement |
| Safety & Facility | Safety incident / critical facility failure | High | Facility safety controls | Periodic inspection | Incident response / CAPA |
| Technical Misjudgment | Incorrect technical evaluation / test decision | High | Technical review | Report review / audit | Technical escalation / CAPA |
| Corrective Action | Repeat findings / overdue CAPA | High | CAPA tracking | Monthly monitoring | Escalation |
| Impartiality | Conflict of interest | High | COI declaration | Periodic assessment | Investigation / escalation |
| Cybersecurity / IP | Unauthorized access / information leakage | Medium | Access control | Security review | Incident response |


Risk levels are indicative and should be determined based on likelihood, impact, and available evidence.
